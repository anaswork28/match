Authenticity Guarantee

Verified Original Products

At Match, authenticity is our top priority. We only sell genuine, original products and never deal in replicas, copies, or counterfeit items.

Every product goes through a quality and authenticity inspection before it is approved for sale on Match.

How to Verify Your Ralph Lauren Shirt

Step 1 – Find the QR Code

Look inside the collar of your shirt, behind the size tag. You will find a unique QR code label attached to the garment.

Step 2 – Scan the QR Code

Open your phone's camera and make sure the lens is clean and focused. Point the camera at the QR code and wait for the official Ralph Lauren verification link to appear.

Step 3 – Confirm Authenticity

Tap the link that appears to open the official Ralph Lauren authentication page. There, you can verify your product through the brand’s verification system.

Each shirt comes with genuine brand labels, authentic details, and an original QR authentication tag.

If the QR Code Does Not Work

In rare cases, the QR code may not open a link because of a minor printing or tag issue. This is uncommon and does not affect the authenticity of the product.

For the best scanning results, make sure your camera lens is clean and properly focused. If the QR code still does not work, you can manually type the official website address printed on the tag into your browser.
