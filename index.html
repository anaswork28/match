Authenticity Guarantee

Verified Original Products

At Match, authenticity is our top priority. We only sell genuine, original products and never deal in replicas, copies, or counterfeit items.

Every product goes through a quality and authenticity inspection before it is approved for sale on Match.

How to Verify Your Ralph Lauren Shirt

Step 1 – Find the QR Code

Look inside the collar of your shirt, behind the size tag. You will find a unique QR code label attached to the garment.

Step 2 – Scan the QR Code

Open your phone's camera and make sure the lens is clean and focused. Point the camera at the QR code and wait for the official Ralph Lauren verification link to appear.

Step 3 – Confirm Authenticity

Tap the link that appears to open the official Ralph Lauren authentication page. You will then be redirected to the Ralph Lauren website.

Each shirt comes with genuine brand labels, authentic details, and an original QR authentication tag.

If the QR Code Does Not Work

In rare cases, the QR code may not open a link because of a minor printing or tag issue. This is uncommon and does not affect the authenticity of the product.

For the best scanning results, make sure your camera lens is clean and properly focused. If the QR code still does not work, you can manually type the official website address printed on the tag into your browser.

Rest assured, every item we sell is authentic and sourced from trusted international suppliers, export surplus channels, and carefully selected premium inventories from around the world.

Returns & Exchanges

Policy & Requirements

Returns and exchanges are accepted within 5 days of receiving your order in the following cases:

  • Incorrect size sent by our team — we will cover the return delivery cost
  • Wrong item delivered — we will cover the return delivery cost
  • Damaged or defective product — we will cover the return delivery cost

Change of Mind / Quality Concerns

If you wish to exchange an item because you are not satisfied with the quality or have simply changed your mind, you will need to courier the item back to us at your own cost.

If you wish to return an item for these reasons, a 20% deduction will apply to the refund, and the return courier cost will be your responsibility.

If a replacement piece is available, we will arrange an exchange accordingly. If the requested item is no longer available, a refund may be issued after the returned item has been inspected and approved.

Return & Exchange Requirements

To qualify for a return or exchange:

  • The item must be unworn and unused
  • Original tags must still be attached
  • Original packaging must be included
  • The product must be returned in its original condition

Items showing signs of wear, washing, damage, or missing tags/packaging may not qualify for return or exchange approval.
